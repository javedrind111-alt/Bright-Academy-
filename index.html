Fee Wasooli —

"Fees Wasool Karein" se poori ya jitni raqam mili ho utni darj karein. Misal: Rs. 500 fee mein Rs. 100 jama hon to Is Mahine Baqi = Rs. 400; agar pichle mahino ka Rs. 2,500 ho to Kul Bakaya = Rs. 2,900. Receipt aur WhatsApp dono mein yeh poori breakdown nazar aayegi. 💬 WhatsApp button Urdu text bhejta hai.
